Who We Are

Mahadeve Multi Speciality Hospital is a patient-focused hospital in Achampet, Nagarkurnool district, started by the husband-and-wife doctors Dr. L. Mahesh and Dr. L. Pavani.

We combine modern techniques with a caring approach, offering general and laparoscopic surgery, surgical care for women's conditions, complete dental treatment and cosmetic care under one roof.

  • Experienced, qualified doctors
  • Clean and hygienic hospital
  • Minimally invasive techniques
  • Clear advice and affordable treatment
Our Mission

To provide safe, ethical and affordable specialist care to every patient in our community.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted multi speciality hospital in the region.

Our Values

Compassion, honesty, safety and respect for every patient and family.
